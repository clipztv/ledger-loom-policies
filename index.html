Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Ledger Loom ("Ledger Loom," "we," "us") sells digital spreadsheet templates through our Etsy shop, LedgerLoomUS. This policy explains what information Ledger Loom collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Ledger Loom does not operate its own checkout or account system. All orders, payments, and buyer messages are handled by Etsy, Inc., and are subject to Etsy's own Privacy Policy. When a customer purchases a Ledger Loom product, Etsy shares limited order information with us (such as the buyer's name, shipping/contact details where applicable, and order contents) so that we can fulfill the order and respond to customer service requests.

How We Use Information

We use the order information provided by Etsy only to fulfill digital product orders, respond to buyer messages, and improve our product listings. Ledger Loom does not sell or rent customer information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Ledger Loom promotes its products on platforms such as Pinterest by linking images ("Pins") to our Etsy listings. Clicking a Pin takes a visitor to Etsy.com, where Etsy's own privacy practices apply. Ledger Loom does not receive personal data from Pinterest about who views or clicks these Pins.

Data Retention

We retain order-related information only as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle customer service, and comply with Etsy's seller policies and applicable law.
